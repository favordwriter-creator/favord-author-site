About Favord

Favord is a writer and poet creating emotionally immersive stories that explore love, grief, obsession, healing, horror, identity, and the hidden corners of the human experience.

Her work blends darkness with beauty, emotion with mystery, and intimacy with the supernatural. Every story is built to be felt, not just read.
